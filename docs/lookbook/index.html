Directions

Three visual systems

Each one is a complete answer: colour, type, shape, and how a phone screen feels in the hand. They are deliberately far apart.

Direction A

Stationery

The invitation suite, made into software. Ivory paper, espresso ink, olive and bronze, a serif with real italics. Hairline rules instead of shadows. Calm, expensive, unhurried.

Direction B

Kanga

Borrowed from the kanga cloth: a bold border band, indigo and marigold and red on unbleached cotton, and a proverb line, the jina, that every real kanga carries. Heavy type, hard edges, confident colour. The only direction that could not have been made for a wedding anywhere else.

Direction C

Ledger

The honest one about what this app mostly is: a shared fifty-thousand-dollar ledger. Dark first, gold-to-coral warmth on deep ink, monospace figures that line up, soft rounded cards. The wedding shows up in the words, not the decoration.
