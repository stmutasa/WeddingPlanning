Directions

Two visual systems

Each one is a complete answer: colour, type, shape, and how a phone screen feels in the hand. Both now carry real colour; they differ in how loud it is and what it means.

Direction A · revised

Stationery, in colour

Same bones as before: ivory paper, espresso ink, a serif with real italics, hairline rules, no shadows. Now with a colour system taken from a Nairobi garden: bougainvillea, marigold, jacaranda, lagoon. Each event owns one colour and keeps it everywhere, so the colour tells you something every time you see it.

Direction B

Kanga

Borrowed from the kanga cloth: a bold border band, indigo and marigold and red on unbleached cotton, and a proverb line, the jina, that every real kanga carries. Heavy type, hard edges, confident colour. The only direction that could not have been made for a wedding anywhere else.
